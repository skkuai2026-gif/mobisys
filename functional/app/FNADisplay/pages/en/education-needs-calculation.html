Education Needs Calculation

Basic Principle:

Required fund will be calculated by each children based upon the present value of projected total expenses (tuition and living) per year for expected years of studying in specified country.

Required Fund
= Present Value [Total expenses per year (including tuition and living) x expected years of studying in specified country] as at the time of entering university

Education Fund
= total amount of education fund prepared by client accumulated with annual rate of return at time to enter university
If there is more than one child, education fund is first allocated to the child closest to entering university followed by the second child closest etc…

Gap
= Required Fund – Education Fund

Required Monthly saving
= monthly saving amount required by clients so as to accumulate the Required Fund for education by the specified investment return rate

Years of saving
= Time period needed to put in saving to accumulate the Required Fund for education. It is also the time until children get into college = (Age to Enter University - Children Age)
