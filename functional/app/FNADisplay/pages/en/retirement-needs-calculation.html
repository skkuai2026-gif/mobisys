Retirement Needs Calculation

Basic Principle

Retirement needs: to maintain quality retirement lifestyle, total retirement fund will consider your Expected retirement expenses for expected years of retirement year and minus MPF contribution.

Required Fund
= Future value [Expected retirement expenses x expected years of retirement year]

MPF
= Future value [MPF monthly contribution (from employee & employer) x 12 months]

Gap
= Required Fund - MPF

Required Monthly saving
= monthly saving amount required by clients so as to accumulate the Required Fund for retirement by the specified investment return rate
