Life Protection Calculation

Basic Principle:

Life protection need = Present value of total financial obligation to your family expenditure in a specified years of supporting plus the immediate cash need. This refers to the lump sum required on premature death contributing to the household so as to maintain the current living standard of your dependents.

Protection Need Calculation Formula:
= Present Value [Total Monthly expenses (Monthly House Rental & Other Monthly Expenses) x 12] over the Expected years of supporting

+ Immediate Cash Need (Mortgage / Education Fund / Funeral Expense / Others)

Liquid Asset
= Cash/Deposit, Time Deposit, Stock, Fund/Bond and Others that the customer already have

Current life Protection
= Total coverage of Life Policies that the customer already possess

Protection Gap
= Protection Need – Liquid Asset – Current Life Protection
