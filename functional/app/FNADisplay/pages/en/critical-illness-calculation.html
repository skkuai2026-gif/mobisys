Critical Illness Calculation1

Basic Principles:

Critical Illness Protection Need = Total protection against income loss, medical expenses, and long term care expenses each with an assumed number of years of support. This refers to the lump sum required in the event of encountering critical illness, to cope with loss of income, medical expenses, and long term care expenses.

Income Replacement2
= Monthly Income X 12 over a specified number of years of loss of income

Medical Expenses3
= Average total expenses of $500,000 (Default: 2 years)

Long Term Care Expenses4
= Average total expenses of $294,000 (Default: 3 years)

Critical Illness Protection Need Calculation Formula:
= Total Value of (Income Replacement2 + Medical Expenses3 + Long Term Care Expenses4)

Current Critical Illness Protection
= Total coverage of Critical Illness Policies that the customer currently possess

Protection Gap
= Critical Illness Protection Need – Current Critical Illness Protection

Notes:
1The Critical Illness Calculation is solely an assumption, based on the private hospital of general ward accommodation with an average projection for reference only. The actual expense is subjected to the circumstances may go up or down.
2To estimate the Critical Illness expense and assume the loss of 100% income, generally it may involve 5-year of curing and recovery period on average.
3On average it may involve 2-year of curing period from the Critical Illness
4On average it may involve 3-year of recovery period from the Critical Illness
